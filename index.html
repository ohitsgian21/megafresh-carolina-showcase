Your corner supermarket, online.

The website I designed and built for Supermercados Mega Fresh Carolina. Shoppers can check the week's specials, flip through the printed shopper, browse every department and reach the store, and a TV mode turns it into signage for the screens inside.

01 — Home

The whole store in one scroll.

  • Specials first. The hero rotates the week's offers and leads straight to them.
  • Departments at a glance. Six areas of the store, each with its own in-aisle clip.
  • Search everywhere. One search box in the header looks across every special and category.
  • Reviews and hours. Customer reviews, opening hours and contact details close the page.

02 — Ofertas

A weekly shopper you can flip through.

  • The circular, on the page. The Mega Fresh shopper appears as an eight-page booklet with pages that turn.
  • Every special, with its photo. All 155 items show size, sale price and regular price, and chips filter them by department.
  • Solo Frío. A toggle shows only chilled items.

03 — Categorías

Walk the aisles before you arrive.

Each department card plays a short clip filmed in the actual aisle, so customers see the real store and not stock photos.

superelmejor.com/categorias
04 — Mobile

Made for the phone in your hand.

Every page reflows for a small screen, menu and search included.

05 — Modo TV

A TV mode for the screens in the store.

  • Full-screen slideshow. The week's specials rotate on their own.
  • Live clock and ticker. Address, phone number and hours scroll along the bottom.
  • One key. Press F on any TV browser to go full screen.
